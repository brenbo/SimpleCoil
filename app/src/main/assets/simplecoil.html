SimpleCoil is an open-source companion app for RECOIL laser tag equipment, designed for fast, reliable gameplay whether playing offline or hosting multiplayer games across large fields.

Features

SimpleCoil works with or without a Wi-Fi hub and includes a wide range of game modes and tactical tools:

  • Game Modes – Play Classic, Domination, Hardpoint, Battle Royale, Capture the Flag, Extraction, Search & Destroy, Infection, and Marksman.
  • Dedicated Server & Live Maps – Host games on Wi-Fi or a RECOIL hub with real-time scoring, live referee maps, post-game heatmaps, and replays.
  • Field Mapping & Satellite Imagery – Draw custom field borders, auto-place respawns, and download satellite imagery (MapTiler, or USGS in the United States) directly to player phones.
  • Supply Drops & Loadouts – Configure in-game crate drops (full auto, salvo, medkits, overshields, UAVs) and custom magazine rules.
  • Instant Pairing & Voice Chat – Connect blasters via Bluetooth or instant QR code scanning, pair grenades, and talk with push-to-talk voice chat.

Quick Controls

  • Tap the Power button to toggle recoil.
  • Tap the Thumb/Weapon Switch button to change fire modes (single, burst, or full auto), or hold it for voice chat / scoreboard.
  • Hold the Reload button while eliminated to respawn.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth – To find and connect to your blaster.
  • Location – To show your position on the in-game map and power GPS game modes. Older Android versions also require it for Bluetooth scanning. Location data is only shared locally with the game server and players in your game.
  • Camera – To scan the QR code on your blaster. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • Local Network / Wi-Fi – To discover and communicate with other players and the game server on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Microphone – To enable push-to-talk voice chat during games.
  • Notifications – To maintain a background service keeping your blaster connected.
  • Vibration – To provide haptic feedback when you take hits.
